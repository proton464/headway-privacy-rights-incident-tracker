# Individual Rights Request & Privacy Incident Workflow Tracker

**Portfolio Project** | Demonstrates triage, statutory deadline management, investigation documentation, and closure for HIPAA/state privacy rights requests and incidents.

## One-Sentence Elevator Pitch
An operational tracker and workflow that shows how to manage individual rights requests and privacy incidents with clear deadlines, investigation notes, and audit-ready evidence.

## Mapping to Headway Privacy Manager JD
| JD Requirement | How This Project Evidences It |
|---------------|--------------------------------|
| Manage HIPAA and state privacy rights workflows (access, amendment, accounting, restriction, deletion) | Rights Requests sheet |
| Triage privacy inquiries, individual rights requests, and potential incidents | Intake fields and status tracking |
| Support incident investigations, documentation, root-cause analysis, and corrective actions | Incidents sheet |
| Track privacy risks and operational metrics including request volume and aging | Dashboard + Aging/Overdue columns |

## Excel Workbook
`Headway_Rights_Incident_Tracker.xlsx` includes sample requests and incidents plus blank rows for additions. Aging and overdue flags calculate automatically.

## Strongest Interview Talking Point
“This tracker shows how I would meet statutory deadlines, document investigations, and maintain audit-ready evidence for rights requests and incidents.”
